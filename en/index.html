Our Technology

Beleza na Capital Platform

The Beleza na Capital Platform is the internal system our team uses to manage our clients' advertising accounts and business profiles.

The platform securely connects to Google Ads and Google Business Profile through Google's official APIs. With each client's authorization, it allows our team to:

  • Track paid traffic campaign performance and generate consolidated reports;
  • Create and adjust campaigns, ad groups, keywords and budgets;
  • Publish updates, hours and review responses on Google Business Profile listings;
  • Monitor spend and results against agreed goals.

Access is restricted to the Beleza na Capital team. We do not sell, share or transfer our clients' account data to third parties. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
